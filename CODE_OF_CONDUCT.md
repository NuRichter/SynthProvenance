# Code of Conduct

SynthProvenance is a research instrument of the NuRichter Workspace research group. Everyone who uses, studies or
contributes to it is expected to uphold both professional conduct and research integrity.

## Professional conduct

- Be respectful and constructive. Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks are not tolerated.
- Assume good faith, give specific and actionable feedback, and credit others' work.
- Respect privacy: do not process images of private individuals without authorisation, and do not add private
  personal names, student identities or unrelated institutional allegiance to public About/README/CITATION text.

## Research integrity

The scientific rules are part of the conduct of this project:

- **We do not guess. We measure.** Every claim must be a measurement with a reproducible record.
- **We do not hide failed experiments. We document them.** Weak and negative results are reported, not dropped.
- Do not fabricate data, citations, DOIs or results. Verified references are marked; unverifiable claims are marked
  UNVERIFIED.
- Do not misrepresent the origin of media, and do not present controlled-surrogate results as proof about real
  generators or real watermarks.
- Keep the terminology distinct (intrinsic vs causal vs spectral vs proactive watermark vs detector representation
  vs C2PA provenance); do not collapse them to reach a conclusion.

## Enforcement

Concerns may be raised privately with the maintainers of the NuRichter Workspace research group. Maintainers may
remove, edit or reject contributions, and may restrict participation, for conduct they judge inappropriate or
scientifically dishonest.
